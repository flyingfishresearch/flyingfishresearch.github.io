Tongji Flying Fish Research Lab welcomes applications from highly motivated prospective Ph.D. and master’s students with strong hands-on engineering skills and an interest in developing aerial-aquatic robots. Researchers interested in postdoctoral opportunities are also encouraged to contact us.

Research Areas

Our research spans fundamental theory and practical applications in the following areas:

  • Mechanical Design & Hydrodynamics
  • Control Theory & Flight Control
  • Sensing & Perception
  • Task & Motion Planning
  • Machine Learning & Embodied AI

Requirements

1. Ph.D. students

  • Academic background: A master’s degree in automation, mechanical engineering, robotics, aerospace engineering, electrical and electronic engineering, or a related discipline. Applicants with a bachelor’s degree in these fields and an outstanding academic record are also welcome. Applications are welcome through the regular admissions process and through recommendation-based direct entry to the Ph.D. program.
  • Skills and experience: A strong interest in research, practical skills in hardware development and programming, and a good command of English. Experience with unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) or underwater robots is a plus.

2. Master’s students

  • Academic background: A bachelor’s degree in automation, mechanical engineering, robotics, aerospace engineering, electrical and electronic engineering, or a related science or engineering discipline. We welcome applicants taking the postgraduate entrance examination and applicants recommended for admission without entrance examinations.
  • Skills and experience: Strong motivation, a keen interest in emerging robotics technologies, and strong practical and programming skills. Prior experience in robotics competitions is a plus.

Note: We plan to recruit 1–2 students with backgrounds in mechanical engineering or electronics. These backgrounds are preferred for some of our student openings but are not required of all applicants.

3. Postdoctoral research opportunities

Researchers interested in postdoctoral opportunities are welcome to contact Prof. Yu Herng Tan to discuss availability, research fit, and position-specific requirements.

How to Apply

If you are interested in joining our lab, please send your application directly to Prof. Yu Herng Tan.

Email: yhtan@tongji.edu.cn

Student applicants should include the following materials:

  1. CV
  2. Academic transcripts (undergraduate and graduate, as applicable)
  3. A brief statement of your research interests or a summary of your previous research

For student applications, please use the email subject format: [Prospective Student] Master's/Ph.D. Application - [Your Name]

同济大学飞鱼研究实验室（Tongji Flying Fish Research Lab）现招收对科研充满热情的博士和硕士研究生。如果您具备较强的动手实践能力，并对跨介质机器人的研发有浓厚兴趣，欢迎申请加入我们的团队攻读硕士或博士学位。我们也欢迎有意开展博士后研究的申请者来信咨询。

研究方向

我们的研究涵盖以下领域的理论探索与工程应用：

  • 机械设计与水动力学
  • 控制理论与飞行控制
  • 传感与感知
  • 任务规划与运动规划
  • 机器学习与具身智能

招生要求

1. 博士研究生

  • 专业背景：已取得自动化、机械工程、机器人学、航空航天、电气与电子工程或相关学科的硕士学位，或为上述领域学业表现优异的本科毕业生。欢迎普通招考或推免直博申请者。
  • 科研能力：具备浓厚的学术兴趣和较强的硬件开发、编程实践能力，英语水平良好。有无人机或水下机器人相关经验者优先。

2. 硕士研究生

  • 专业背景：已取得自动化、机械工程、机器人学、航空航天、电气与电子工程或相关学科的学士学位。欢迎硕士统考及推免申请者。
  • 科研能力：自我驱动力强，对机器人前沿技术充满热情，具备扎实的动手实践能力和编程能力。有机器人竞赛经验者优先。

说明：我们计划招收 1–2 名具有机械工程或电子专业背景的学生。上述背景仅为部分招生名额的优先考虑条件，并非所有申请者的必备条件。

3. 博士后研究机会

有意开展博士后研究的申请者，欢迎联系Yu Herng Tan 老师，咨询岗位情况、研究方向及申请条件。

申请方式

如果您有兴趣加入我们，请直接将申请材料发送至 Yu Herng Tan 老师的电子邮箱。

Email: yhtan@tongji.edu.cn

研究生申请者请在邮件中附上以下材料：

  1. 个人简历
  2. 本科及硕士阶段（如适用）的成绩单
  3. 简短的研究兴趣说明或以往研究成果介绍

研究生申请邮件的主题格式请使用： [申请] 硕士/博士申请 - [您的姓名]
